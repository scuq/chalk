# Runestone v2.1 — Distinct Fantasy UI

Format: stereo WAV, 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM. This revision keeps the nine approved Runestone v2 cues and replaces new message with a much shorter, brighter metallic fantasy spark. Its broad timing and spectral character are informed by iTellMessage.ogg, but the waveform is newly synthesized and does not reproduce the recording.

| Event group | Sound family |
|---|---|
| Presence | Short horn and bell flourish |
| Your call state | Large magical portal opening/closing |
| Other people in call | Physical wooden knocks and parchment |
| Connection state | Metal chain/rune bell or chain/drum |
| Send confirmation | Tiny parchment-and-wood action |
| Error | Organic fantasy-creature texture with low drum |
| New message | Compact high-frequency metallic fantasy spark |

Files: 01 friend online, 02 you join call, 03 you leave call, 04 someone joins, 05 someone leaves, 06 connected, 07 disconnected, 08 send confirmed, 09 error, 10 new message.
