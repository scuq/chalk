# Empire Sound Theme

Format: stereo WAV, 48 kHz, PCM (16-bit; cues 02 and 10 are 24-bit). A medieval real-time-strategy palette of synthesized horn, timber, metal, parchment, and drum cues. Cues 02 and 10 originally derived from two supplied reference recordings; both were replaced with newly synthesized versions before the theme shipped.

| File | Event | Source/design |
|---|---|---|
| 01_friend_online.wav | Friend online | Original short horn-and-metal welcome. |
| 02_you_join_call.wav | Enter channel / you join call | Synthesized chant-and-horn fanfare (replaces an earlier conversion of a supplied recording). |
| 03_you_leave_call.wav | You leave call | Original gate-close, timber, and falling horn. |
| 04_someone_joins.wav | Someone joins | Original paired wooden arrival knocks. |
| 05_someone_leaves.wav | Someone leaves | Original inverse wooden departure knocks. |
| 06_connected.wav | Connected | Original paired blacksmith-metal confirmation. |
| 07_disconnected.wav | Disconnected | Original timber, broken metal, and low drum. |
| 08_send_confirmed.wav | Send confirmed | Original parchment-and-wood click. |
| 09_error.wav | Error | Original low war drum and falling horn. |
| 10_new_message.wav | New message received | Synthesized message cue (replaces an earlier excerpt of a supplied recording). |

Rights note: every cue in this folder is newly synthesized. The two supplied
reference recordings that earlier versions of cues 02 and 10 derived from
(an AoE2 "wololo" sample and a YouTube excerpt) do not ship; their
replacements are original waveforms informed by, not reproducing, those
references.
