## Why

Two things at once.

**Calls were silent about themselves.** Joining, leaving, and someone else
arriving or leaving the room you are in all happened with no audible signal —
in a call you are often not looking at the window. Yours is a warm two-stroke
mark, theirs a single light one, each pair rising on the way in and falling on
the way out, so you can tell who it was about without looking.

**The sound pack did not sound like chalk.** The phase-40 strokes were smooth
swishes — a filtered hiss. Real chalk has stick-slip: an irregular crumble as it
drags, opening with the light tick of contact. Getting there meant a **pink**
noise source rather than white, which changes the perceived energy of every
existing sound and therefore forces every gain trim to be re-derived by ear.

That is the reason 71-2 exists: `tools/sound-bench.mjs` builds the bench a
listening session needs — every category, the real synth graph extracted from
source so it cannot drift, sliders, A/B against what is committed, and the
`synth.test.ts` invariants shown live so tuning cannot end in a red build. Its
"copy tuned specs" block pastes back into the table.

## What landed

- **71-1** — call join/leave and peer arrival/departure sounds, generated by the
  chalk-stroke pack; all four on by default, individually switchable under
  "chalk's own noises".
- **71-2** — the sound bench.
- **71-3** — stick-slip grain, contact tick, and a pink source for the pack.
- **71-4** — match the pink source to white's energy; re-derive the gain trims.

## Rule

**Never derive these numbers.** `SOUND_SPECS` is the recording of a listening
session; each comment says why its value is what it is. Changing one means
listening again and rewriting the comment with it.
